### FAQ 1
Title: What is the best time to visit Panna Tiger Reserve?
Content:
The best period for wildlife sightings is from November to June. Winter offers pleasant weather, while summer increases waterhole sightings.

Title: How many safaris should I book for good sightings?
Content:
For better probability, we recommend at least 2 safaris (one sunrise and one evening), especially for 2-night or longer stays.
